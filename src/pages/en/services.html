Scalable · High Performance · High Availability

A Magento system that scales and stays online

Most Magento slowdowns don't come from Magento itself. They come from cache misses, under-sized search, a single database node, or a deployment that takes the store offline. We design the whole stack for speed and uptime from the start.

We run Magento on AWS with Kubernetes, put Varnish in front of every page that can be cached, move cache and sessions to Redis and tune OpenSearch for your catalog. Then we load-test it before your next campaign does.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what Magento store owners usually ask us first.

Which Magento versions do you work with?

Magento 2 Open Source and Adobe Commerce. We build new stores, take over existing ones, upgrade older Magento 2 versions and migrate stores from other platforms.

Can you take over a store built by another agency?

Yes. We start with an audit of the code, extensions, infrastructure and release process, so we know the risks before we change anything. Then we document what we find and agree on priorities with you.

Do we have to move to AWS and Kubernetes?

No. We recommend infrastructure based on your traffic, catalog size and budget. Often the first gains come from fixing caching, search and slow code on your current hosting. We only propose a move when the numbers justify it.

How do you use AI without putting our code or data at risk?

We agree with you upfront which AI tools may be used on your code and data. AI helps us read code, draft changes and write tests, but every change is reviewed by an engineer and goes through the same tests and release process as any other.

What does the free Magento audit include?

A review of performance (profiled with New Relic or Tideways), infrastructure, caching, search, extensions and your release process. You get a written, prioritized list of fixes, whether or not you work with us afterwards.

How do engagements and pricing work?

Three models: a fixed-scope audit, project delivery with an agreed scope, or a dedicated Magento team billed monthly. After a short call we send an estimate based on your actual store.
