Why Magento Only

Depth beats breadth when uptime is on the line

Magento is powerful and unforgiving. Its plugin system, indexers, caching layers and deployment modes all affect how fast and how stable a store is. Generalist agencies often learn this on a client's live store.

We take the opposite approach. Our engineers specialize in Magento, use the same production stack across projects, and treat performance, testing and monitoring as part of the job, not as extras. AI tools make the team faster, and engineering judgment keeps the work safe.

Part of Open Techiz

The Magento team of a 60-engineer company

Magentiz is the Magento team of Open Techiz, a product engineering company with 10 years of experience and 60 engineers, serving SMEs and global brands across Europe, Australia and the United States.

How We Operate

Principles we
work by

01

Every recommendation starts with data from New Relic, Tideways or Grafana. If we can't measure an improvement, we don't claim it.

02

Deployments, regression tests and health checks run automatically, so problems aren't left to someone remembering to check.

Servers fail and traffic spikes. We build stores that stay up when either happens.

We use Claude, OpenAI and Antigravity wherever they help. An engineer reviews every line that ships.

Your code, infrastructure and dashboards stay yours. Everything is documented so you're never locked in.
