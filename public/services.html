Scalable · High Performance · High Availability

A Magento system that scales and stays online

Most Magento slowdowns don't come from Magento itself. They come from cache misses, under-sized search, a single database node, or a deployment that takes the store offline. We design the whole stack for speed and uptime from the start.

We run Magento on AWS with Kubernetes, put Varnish in front of every page that can be cached, move cache and sessions to Redis and tune OpenSearch for your catalog. Then we load-test it before your next campaign does.

High-Availability Architecture

Multi-AZ on AWS, replicated database, horizontal PHP-FPM pods on Kubernetes. No single node can take the store down.

Caching That Actually Hits

Varnish full-page cache with correct hole-punching, Redis for cache and sessions, and cache warming after deploys.

Fast Catalog Search

OpenSearch sized, indexed and tuned for large catalogs, multiple store views and fast layered navigation.

Ready for Peak Traffic

Autoscaling rules, load tests that replay real traffic, and a peak-season runbook agreed with your team in advance.

Zero-Downtime Deployments

Blue/green or rolling releases on Kubernetes, safe database migrations and instant rollback. You won't need a maintenance page again.

Magento Team with Applied AI Skills

Magento engineers who work with AI every day

AI only helps if the people using it understand the platform. Our Magento engineers use Claude, OpenAI models and Google Antigravity throughout the delivery cycle, from reading legacy code to generating tests. A senior engineer reviews everything before it reaches your store.

In practice this means shorter upgrade projects, better test coverage, faster onboarding to existing codebases, and AI features that are ready for production.

Claude OpenAI Antigravity
How We Use AI
  • AI-Assisted Development & Code Review Claude and Antigravity agents help us understand unfamiliar modules, draft code that follows Magento coding standards, and catch issues in review before a human signs off.
  • Faster Upgrades & Broader Test Coverage AI-driven analysis of deprecated APIs and extension conflicts during Magento upgrades, plus generated Selenium scenarios that cover more of the store.
  • AI Features for Your Store Semantic product search on OpenSearch, AI-written product content, and support assistants built on Claude or OpenAI APIs, all connected to your Magento data.
  • Smarter Operations AI summaries of New Relic and Grafana incidents and faster root-cause analysis, so on-call engineers spend their time fixing problems instead of searching logs.
