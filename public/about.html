Why Magento Only

Depth beats breadth when uptime is on the line

Magento is powerful and unforgiving. Its plugin system, indexers, caching layers and deployment modes all affect how fast and how stable a store is. Generalist agencies often learn this on a client's live store.

We take the opposite approach. Our engineers specialize in Magento, use the same production stack across projects, and treat performance, testing and monitoring as part of the job, not as extras. AI tools make the team faster, and engineering judgment keeps the work safe.
